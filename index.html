ESP-FC Flasher

Flash esp-fc flight-controller firmware to an ESP board, then configure it with Betaflight Configurator 10.10. Needs desktop Chrome or Edge (Web Serial).

This browser has no Web Serial. Open the page in desktop Chrome or Edge. Firefox and Safari can't flash.

1Choose your board

esp-fc is built per chip, not per dev kit. Any board with a supported chip and at least 4 MB flash can run it. The board choice just selects the right chip and shows the matching caveats. Pin defaults are the firmware's, so wire to them or remap with the CLI.

Board won't connect? Hold BOOT, tap EN/RESET, release BOOT, then click again. Boards with native USB (S2/S3/C3) often need this. Flashing starts at 0x00. The page refuses to flash if the connected chip differs from the one you picked. After success, unplug and replug power.

5ESC flashing / rewriting

    
Experimental in esp-fc. The author added a BLHeli 4-way passthrough in v0.2.0-RC4 and labels it experimental. This page can't write ESC firmware directly, because the flight controller has to bridge the serial lines. Use a browser ESC tool through the FC.

Before any ESC firmware write: confirm the exact ESC model and MCU family in the tool, and use firmware built for that model. A wrong image can leave an ESC dead. If the tool can't see the ESCs, esp-fc's passthrough isn't working for your setup, so flash them with a different FC or a dedicated USB link.

    
To spin a motor the other way, swapping two of its three wires also works, and esp-fc's setup guide recommends it.
